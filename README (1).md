### 🔭 What I'm Working On

I build deep learning systems for **space weather forecasting** at **NIT Delhi**. My current research focuses on predicting solar flares before they disrupt satellite communications and power grids.

**Dynoflare-net** — my TCN-with-attention model — achieves **TSS 0.79** and **AUC 0.96** on the SWAN-SF benchmark for M/X-class solar flare prediction.
